Recommended Settings for Juggernaut-X-Hyper

Sampling Steps

20-30

Higher values (25-30) for more detailed images. For simpler images, 20 steps is often sufficient.

CFG Scale

7-9

Controls how closely the image follows your prompt. Higher values (10+) can cause artifacts.

Sampling Method

DPM++ 2M Karras

Best overall quality. Alternative: Euler a (faster) or DPM++ SDE Karras (more detailed).

Resolution

1024×1024

Optimal for most cases. For landscapes, try 1152×896. For portraits, try 896×1152.

Seed

-1 (Random)

Use -1 for exploration. Save seeds of images you like to recreate similar results later.

Denoising Strength

0.6-0.8

For img2img. Lower (0.4-0.6) preserves more of original; higher (0.7-0.9) for more creativity.
